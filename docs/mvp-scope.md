## OUT OF SCOPE — v1

All indoor materials are deferred until the MVP ships:
- Paint
- Flooring / laminate / vinyl
- Tile
- Drywall
- Wallpaper
- Insulation

Also deferred:
- Affiliate links (add after traffic is established)
- Per-dimension pages (e.g. "how many bags for a 12x12 slab") — scaled-content risk
- Any material not listed above

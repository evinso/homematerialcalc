## Formula by shape

### Rectangle or square
**Area = Length × Width**  
*Example: 12 ft × 15 ft = 180 sq ft*

### L-shaped room
**Area = Rectangle 1 + Rectangle 2**  
Split into two rectangles, calculate each, add together.

### Triangle
**Area = (Base × Height) ÷ 2**  
*Example: 8 ft base × 6 ft height ÷ 2 = 24 sq ft*

### Circle
**Area = π × radius²** (π ≈ 3.14159)  
*Example: 5 ft radius → 3.14159 × 25 = 78.5 sq ft*

## How to convert feet and inches

When your measurements include inches, convert to decimal feet:

| Inches | Decimal feet |
|--------|-------------|
| 3" | 0.25 |
| 4" | 0.33 |
| 6" | 0.50 |
| 8" | 0.67 |
| 9" | 0.75 |
| 10" | 0.83 |

*Example: 11 ft 6 in × 14 ft 9 in = 11.5 × 14.75 = 169.6 sq ft*

## Step-by-step for a full house

1. **Sketch each room** — rough floor plan with all dimensions
2. **Measure each room** — length and width in feet and inches
3. **Calculate each room** — length × width
4. **Add all rooms together** — total square footage
5. **Add waste factor** — 10% for flooring, 15% for tile

## Subtract doors and windows for paint

For painting, measure wall area (not floor area):
- Wall area = Perimeter × Wall height
- Subtract: each door ≈ 20 sq ft, each window ≈ 15 sq ft

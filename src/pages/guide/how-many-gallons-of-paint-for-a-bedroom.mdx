## Gallons needed by bedroom size (2 coats, 8 ft ceiling, standard paint)

| Room size | Wall area | Doors | Windows | Net area | Gallons |
|-----------|-----------|-------|---------|----------|---------|
| 10 × 10 ft | 320 sq ft | 1 | 1 | 284 sq ft | **2 gal** |
| 10 × 12 ft | 352 sq ft | 1 | 2 | 301 sq ft | **2 gal** |
| 12 × 12 ft | 384 sq ft | 1 | 2 | 333 sq ft | **2–3 gal** |
| 12 × 14 ft | 416 sq ft | 1 | 2 | 365 sq ft | **3 gal** |
| 12 × 16 ft | 448 sq ft | 1 | 2 | 397 sq ft | **3 gal** |
| 14 × 16 ft | 480 sq ft | 1 | 2 | 429 sq ft | **3 gal** |

*Net area = wall area minus 21 sq ft per door and 15 sq ft per window. Gallons rounded up.*

## With 9 or 10 ft ceilings

Taller ceilings add significant wall area — roughly 12–15% more per foot of extra height.

| Room | 8 ft ceiling | 9 ft ceiling | 10 ft ceiling |
|------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| 10 × 12 ft | 2 gal | 2–3 gal | 3 gal |
| 12 × 12 ft | 2–3 gal | 3 gal | 3 gal |
| 12 × 16 ft | 3 gal | 3–4 gal | 4 gal |

| Trim paint | 1 quart (semi-gloss or satin) |
| Ceiling paint | 1 quart (1 coat) or 1 gallon (2 coats) |

## Is DIY insulation worth it?

Blown-in attic insulation is one of the few home improvement projects where DIY almost always makes sense:

- **Machine rental:** Most stores offer free blower rental with purchase of insulation bags
- **Time:** A 1,000 sq ft attic takes 4–6 hours with two people
- **Savings:** DIY saves $800–$2,000 vs. professional installation on a typical attic

Wall and spray foam insulation are better left to pros due to precision requirements and equipment costs.

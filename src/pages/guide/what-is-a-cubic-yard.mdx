## The basics

| Fact | Value |
|------|-------|
| 1 cubic yard | 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft |
| 1 cubic yard | **27 cubic feet** |
| 1 cubic yard | 46,656 cubic inches |
| 1 cubic yard | 0.7646 cubic meters |

Think of it as a cube about the size of a standard washing machine or a large dresser.

## How much does a cubic yard weigh?

| Material | Weight per cubic yard |
|----------|----------------------|
| Mulch (shredded) | 400–800 lbs |
| Topsoil (dry) | ~2,200 lbs (1.1 tons) |
| Sand | 2,600–2,700 lbs (1.30–1.35 tons) |
| Gravel / crushed stone | 2,700–3,000 lbs (1.35–1.50 tons) |
| Concrete (wet mix) | ~4,050 lbs (2.0 tons) |

## How much area does a cubic yard cover?

| Material | Depth | Coverage |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Mulch | 2 inches | 162 sq ft |
| Mulch | 3 inches | 108 sq ft |
| Topsoil | 4 inches | 81 sq ft |
| Gravel | 2 inches | 162 sq ft |
| Gravel | 4 inches | 81 sq ft |

**Formula:** Coverage (sq ft) = 324 ÷ Depth (in)

## How many bags equal a cubic yard?

| Material | Bag size | Bags per cubic yard |
|----------|----------|-------------------|
| Mulch | 2 cu ft | 13.5 bags |
| Topsoil | 40 lb (0.75 cu ft) | 36 bags |
| Sand | 50 lb (0.50 cu ft) | 54 bags |
| Concrete | 80 lb (0.60 cu ft) | 45 bags |

- **9 wheelbarrow loads** (standard 3 cu ft contractor barrow)
- A cube **3 feet tall × 3 feet wide × 3 feet deep**
- About the size of a **standard washing machine**
- A pickup truck bed holds roughly **1–2 cubic yards** (depending on truck size and material density)

## The formula

**Square feet = Linear feet × Width (ft)**

**Linear feet = Square feet ÷ Width (ft)**

*Width must be in feet — divide inches by 12 first.*

## Fencing: linear feet vs. board feet

For a 6-foot tall privacy fence with 6-inch boards:

| Fence length | Boards needed | Linear feet of boards |
|-------------|--------------|----------------------|
| 50 lin ft | 50 × 12 boards/section | 600 linear feet |
| 100 lin ft | 200 boards | 1,200 linear feet |

## Baseboard and trim

Linear feet of trim = Room perimeter (ft) minus door openings (typically 3 ft each)

For a 12×14 ft room:
Perimeter = (12+14) × 2 = 52 ft
Minus 2 doors = 52 − 6 = **46 linear feet of baseboard**

## Lumber: linear feet to board feet

**Board feet = Linear feet × Thickness (in) × Width (in) ÷ 12**

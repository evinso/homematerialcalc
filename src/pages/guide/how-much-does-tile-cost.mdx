## Key cost factors

- **Tile size** — Large-format tile (18×18+) costs more to install ($1–$3/sq ft extra) due to leveling requirements
- **Grout joint width** — Wider joints need more grout; rectified tile with tight joints (1/16") reduces grout cost
- **Substrate** — Cement board adds $0.50–$1.00/sq ft vs. direct-bond to concrete
- **Pattern** — Diagonal or herringbone patterns waste 15% vs. 10% for straight lay, increasing material cost

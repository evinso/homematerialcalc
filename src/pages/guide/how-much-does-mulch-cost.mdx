## Bulk mulch prices by type

| Mulch type | Cost per cubic yard (bulk) |
|------------|---------------------------|
| Wood chips (basic) | $20–$35 |
| Shredded hardwood bark | $30–$50 |
| Dyed / colored mulch | $35–$65 |
| Cedar mulch | $40–$75 |
| Pine bark nuggets | $35–$55 |
| Pine straw (per bale) | $5–$8 / bale |
| Rubber mulch (playground) | $80–$160 |

*Bulk prices are for pick-up at landscape supply yards. Add $30–$75 for delivery.*

## Bagged mulch prices (big-box stores)

| Bag size | Price range | Cost per cubic yard equivalent |
|----------|-------------|-------------------------------|
| 2 cu ft bag | $4–$8 | $70–$140 / cu yd |
| 3 cu ft bag | $6–$12 | $70–$130 / cu yd |

**Conclusion:** Bags cost 2–3× more per cubic yard than bulk. Buy bulk for any order over 2 yards.

## Professional installation costs

| Service | Cost range |
|---------|-----------|
| Labor only (per yard installed) | $20–$35 |
| Materials + labor (per yard) | $55–$95 |
| Minimum job fee | $75–$200 |
| Full yard mulching (3 cu yd job) | $150–$400 |

## Total cost examples

| Job size | DIY (bulk) | Professional |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Small yard — 2 yards | $60–$120 + delivery | $120–$200 |
| Medium yard — 4 yards | $120–$240 + delivery | $220–$380 |
| Large yard — 8 yards | $240–$480 + delivery | $440–$760 |

## How to save on mulch

1. **Order direct from a landscape supply yard** — avoid the retail markup on bagged mulch
2. **Buy in fall** — many suppliers discount overstock mulch in late October and November
3. **Share a bulk delivery** — split a 10-yard delivery with a neighbor to share the delivery fee

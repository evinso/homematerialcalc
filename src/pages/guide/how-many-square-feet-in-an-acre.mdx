## The exact conversion

**1 acre = 43,560 square feet**

**1 acre = 4,840 square yards**

**1 acre = 0.405 hectares**

## Formulas

**Acres → square feet:** Acres × 43,560 = sq ft

**Square feet → acres:** Sq ft ÷ 43,560 = acres

**Example:** A 2.5 acre parcel = 2.5 × 43,560 = **108,900 sq ft**

## Using this for landscaping

If you are seeding or sodding a large lawn, you need to know your area in square feet. Measure with a GPS app (most phones have one) or multiply the rough dimensions.

**Example:** A 1.5-acre lawn = 1.5 × 43,560 = 65,340 sq ft

At 7 lbs of tall fescue seed per 1,000 sq ft: 65,340 ÷ 1,000 × 7 = **457 lbs of seed**

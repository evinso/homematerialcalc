## The answer at a glance — 100 sq ft

| Depth | Cu ft | Cu yards | 2 cu ft bags (w/ 5% waste) |
|-------|-------|----------|---------------------------|
| 1 inch | 8.3 | 0.31 | 5 bags |
| 2 inches | 16.7 | 0.62 | 10 bags |
| **3 inches** | **25.0** | **0.93** | **14 bags** |
| 4 inches | 33.3 | 1.23 | 18 bags |
| 6 inches | 50.0 | 1.85 | 27 bags |

## How we calculated it

For 100 sq ft at 3 inches:
1. Volume = 100 × 3 ÷ 12 = 25 cu ft
2. Cu yd = 25 ÷ 27 = 0.926 cu yd
3. Bags = 0.926 × 13.5 = 12.5 → **13 bags** (without waste)
4. With 5% waste: 12.5 × 1.05 = 13.1 → **14 bags**

## When to buy bulk vs. bags

For 100 sq ft at 3 inches (14 bags), buying bags is convenient. At 13.5 bags per cubic yard, you need just under 1 full yard — buying bags from a garden center is easier than ordering a partial yard of bulk mulch. If you have multiple beds totaling 200+ sq ft, compare bulk delivery prices.

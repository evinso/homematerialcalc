## Quick answer

| Bag size | Bags per cubic yard |
|----------|-------------------|
| 50 lb bag | **54 bags** |
| 60 lb bag | **45 bags** |
| 80 lb bag | **36 bags** |

*1 cubic yard of sand ≈ 2,700 lbs ≈ 1.35 tons*

## How this works

1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet
1 bag of 50 lb sand ≈ 0.5 cubic feet
27 ÷ 0.5 = **54 bags**

## Bags vs. bulk sand: when to switch

| Amount needed | Best to buy |
|--------------|-------------|
| Under 0.25 cu yd (13 bags) | Bags — convenient, no minimum order |
| 0.25–0.75 cu yd | Either — compare price |
| Over 0.75 cu yd | Bulk delivery usually saves money |

**Average pricing:**
- 50 lb bag: $5–$9 at hardware stores
- Bulk washed sand: $25–$45 per cubic yard delivered

At 54 bags × $7 = $378 vs. bulk at $45/cu yd — bulk is far cheaper for large projects.

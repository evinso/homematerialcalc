## Shower wall tile estimate

For a standard 36×36 inch shower with 8-foot ceilings:
- 3 walls × (3 ft wide × 8 ft tall) = 72 sq ft (approx, before door/window deductions)
- With 15% waste: ~83 sq ft
- In 12×12 tiles: **83 tiles**

## Bags per cubic yard — all sizes

| Bag size | Yield per bag | Bags per cubic yard |
|----------|--------------|---------------------|
| 80 lb | 0.60 cu ft | **45 bags** |
| 60 lb | 0.45 cu ft | **60 bags** |
| 50 lb | 0.375 cu ft | **72 bags** |
| 40 lb | 0.30 cu ft | **90 bags** |

*Per Quikrete manufacturer specifications. 1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet.*

## Which bag size should you choose?

- **80 lb bags** — best yield per bag, least bags to buy and mix. Good for most DIY projects.
- **60 lb bags** — easier to carry solo, decent yield. Best for working alone.
- **50 lb bags** — less common; similar to 60 lb in convenience.
- **40 lb bags** — easiest to handle; takes 2× as many bags as 80 lb. Best for small, delicate jobs.

## The math behind the numbers

1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet.

Each bag yields a specific volume when mixed with water per the manufacturer's instructions:

- 80 lb bag: 27 ÷ 0.60 = 45
- 60 lb bag: 27 ÷ 0.45 = 60
- 40 lb bag: 27 ÷ 0.30 = 90

## Formula

**Square yards = Square feet ÷ 9**

**Square feet = Square yards × 9**

## When you'll see square yards

- **Carpet:** sold by the square yard at most flooring stores
- **Turf / artificial grass:** often priced per square yard
- **Sod:** some suppliers price in square yards (1 sq yd = 9 sq ft)
- **Landscaping fabric:** some rolls listed in square yards

For tile, hardwood, and LVP, measurements are almost always in square feet. Use the [Flooring Calculator](/calculator/flooring) or [Tile Calculator](/calculator/tile) for those.

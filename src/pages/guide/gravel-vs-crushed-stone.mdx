## Key differences

| Feature | Natural Gravel | Crushed Stone |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| Edge shape | Rounded (smooth) | Angular (sharp) |
| How it's made | Natural water erosion | Machine-crushed rock |
| Compaction | Poor — shifts and moves | Good — locks together |
| Drainage | Excellent | Good |
| Best use | Drainage, decorative, French drains | Driveways, base layers, paths |

## Common types and their uses

| Name | Type | Size | Best used for |
|------|------|------|--------------|
| Pea gravel | Natural | ¼"–½" | Paths, playgrounds, landscaping |
| River rock | Natural | ½"–4" | Drainage, decorative, dry creek beds |
| #57 stone | Crushed | ¾" | Drainage, French drains, base layer |
| #67 stone | Crushed | ¾"–1" | Drainage, pipe bedding |
| Crusher run | Crushed | 0"–¾" | Driveway base, compacting sub-base |
| Decomposed granite | Crushed | Fine | Paths, patios, xeriscape |
| Washed clean stone | Crushed | ½"–1" | French drains, under slabs |

## What to use for each project

### Driveway
**Layer 1 (base):** 4–6 inches of crusher run, compacted  
**Layer 2 (surface):** 2–3 inches of 1½" or ¾" crushed stone  
Avoid pea gravel or river rock on driveways — they don't compact and will rut.

### French drain
**#57 washed stone or ¾" clean crushed stone.** Angular edges prevent migration into the drain pipe while allowing maximum water flow.

### Walkways and paths
**Pea gravel or decomposed granite** — comfortable underfoot, visually appealing.

### Under pavers or concrete slabs
**Crusher run or #57 stone** — compacts to form a stable, level base.

### Decorative landscaping
**River rock or pea gravel** — looks natural, available in many colors and sizes.

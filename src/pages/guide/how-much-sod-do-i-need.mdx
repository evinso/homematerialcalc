## Sod basics

| Unit | Coverage |
|------|----------|
| 1 piece (standard) | 16" × 24" = **2.67 sq ft** |
| 1 pallet | ~170 pieces = **~450 sq ft** |
| Waste (straight edges) | **+5%** |
| Waste (curved / irregular) | **+10%** |

## The quick formula

**Pieces needed = Area (sq ft) × (1 + waste) ÷ 2.67, rounded up**

**Pallets needed = Area (sq ft) × (1 + waste) ÷ 450, rounded up**

## How to measure your lawn

1. **Sketch your lawn** — draw a rough outline on paper.
2. **Break it into rectangles** — divide irregular shapes into simpler rectangular sections.
3. **Measure each section** — length × width in feet.
4. **Subtract hardscape** — driveways, patios, walkways, garden beds.
5. **Add waste** — 5% for clean straight edges, 10% for curves.

## What to order alongside sod

- **Topsoil:** Prepare 4–6 inches of good topsoil before laying sod. Use the [Topsoil Calculator](/calculator/topsoil).
- **Starter fertilizer:** Apply before laying sod for faster establishment.
- **Irrigation:** Sod needs daily watering for the first 2 weeks.

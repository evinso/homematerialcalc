## The conversion

**1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet (exact)**

**Cu yd = Cu ft ÷ 27**

**Cu ft = Cu yd × 27**

## How to calculate volume from area + depth

If you know area (sq ft) and depth (inches):

**Cu ft = Area (sq ft) × Depth (in) ÷ 12**

**Cu yd = Cu ft ÷ 27 = Area × Depth (in) ÷ 324**

Example: 100 sq ft at 3 inches = 100 × 3 ÷ 324 = **0.93 cubic yards**

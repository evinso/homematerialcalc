## The quick formula

**Mulch needed (cu yd) = Length (ft) × Width (ft) × Depth (in) ÷ 324**

Then multiply by 13.5 to convert cubic yards to 2 cu ft bags.

## Step-by-step: how to measure your beds

1. **Measure each bed** — Walk the perimeter with a tape measure. Record length and width in feet. For curved beds, break them into rough rectangles.
2. **Add up your areas** — For multiple beds, measure each one separately and add them together.
3. **Choose a depth** — 2–3 inches is standard for existing beds; 3–4 inches for new beds.
4. **Plug into the formula** — Or use the [Mulch Calculator](/calculator/mulch) to get bags and cubic yards instantly.

## Coverage table — how far does 1 cubic yard go?

| Depth | Coverage per cubic yard |
|-------|------------------------|
| 1 inch | 324 sq ft |
| 2 inches | 162 sq ft |

| 6 inches | 54 sq ft |

*1 cubic yard = 13.5 bags (2 cu ft each)*

## Bags vs. bulk: which should you buy?

- **Under 1 cubic yard (13 bags):** bags from a garden center are usually more convenient.
- **1–3 cubic yards:** compare bag cost vs. bulk delivery — bulk often wins above 1.5 yards.
- **Over 3 cubic yards:** bulk delivery is almost always cheaper and less work.

## How much does mulch cost?

Bagged mulch typically runs **$4–$8 per 2 cu ft bag** at big-box stores. Bulk delivery varies by region: **$25–$60 per cubic yard** is typical. Enter your local price into the [Mulch Calculator](/calculator/mulch) for a cost estimate.

## Formula

**Tons = Cubic yards × Density (tons/cu yd)**

**Cubic yards = Tons ÷ Density (tons/cu yd)**

## Worked examples

**Example 1:** You need 3 cubic yards of pea gravel (1.40 t/cu yd).
→ 3 × 1.40 = **4.20 tons**

**Example 2:** Your supplier delivers in tons. You need to cover 200 sq ft at 3" with crushed stone.
→ Volume = 200 × 3 ÷ 324 = 1.85 cu yd → 1.85 × 1.40 = **2.59 tons** (order 3 tons)

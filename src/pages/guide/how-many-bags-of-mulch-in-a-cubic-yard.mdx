## The answer

**1 cubic yard = 13.5 bags of 2 cu ft mulch**

This comes from a simple conversion: 1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet. Divide by the bag size:

> 27 cu ft ÷ 2 cu ft per bag = **13.5 bags**

Since you cannot buy half a bag, always round up to **14 bags** when purchasing.

## Conversion table: bags per cubic yard by bag size

| Bag size | Bags per cubic yard |
|----------|-------------------|
| 2 cu ft (standard) | **13.5 bags** → order 14 |
| 3 cu ft | **9 bags** |
| 1.5 cu ft | **18 bags** |

## When to buy bags vs. bulk

- **Bags:** Best for under 1.5 cu yd, tight-access locations, or exact color matching.
- **Bulk delivery:** Best for over 1.5–2 cubic yards. A cubic yard of bulk mulch costs roughly the same as 10–12 bags — saving you 20–40%.

## The answer

| Unit | Weight |
|------|--------|
| 1 cubic yard (dry topsoil) | **~2,200 lbs (1.1 tons)** |
| 1 cubic foot | ~81 lbs |
| 1 cubic yard (wet/clay) | 2,500–3,000 lbs |
| 40 lb bag (0.75 cu ft) | 40 lbs |

*Source: Soil supplier industry standard. Moisture content is the biggest variable.*

## Can I haul topsoil myself?

- **Half-ton pickup (1/2 T):** safely carry ~1,000 lbs — under half a cubic yard
- **3/4-ton pickup:** safely carry ~1,500 lbs — about 2/3 of a cubic yard
- **1-ton pickup:** safely carry ~2,000 lbs — close to a full cubic yard

Always stay within your truck's payload rating (listed in the door jamb sticker). Overloading damages tires, suspension, and brakes.

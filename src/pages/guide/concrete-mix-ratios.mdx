## Standard mix ratios

| Mix | Cement : Sand : Aggregate | PSI result | Best for |
|-----|--------------------------|------------|---------|
| Lean mix | 1 : 3 : 6 | ~2,000 | Fill, blinding layer |
| Standard | 1 : 2 : 3 | ~3,000 | General slabs, sidewalks |
| Strong mix | 1 : 1.5 : 3 | ~3,500 | Driveways, footings |
| Rich mix | 1 : 1 : 2 | ~4,000+ | Structural, heavy duty |
| Mortar (no aggregate) | 1 : 3 | N/A | Brickwork, joints |

## PSI requirements by application

| Application | Minimum PSI | Recommended PSI |
|-------------|------------|----------------|
| Sidewalks, paths | 2,500 | 3,000 |
| Patio slabs | 2,500 | 3,000–3,500 |
| Residential driveway | 3,000 | 4,000 |
| Garage floor | 3,500 | 4,000 |
| Footings | 2,500 | 3,500 |
| Foundation walls | 3,000 | 4,000 |
| Heavy truck driveway | 4,000 | 4,500 |

## Water-to-cement ratio

The most important factor in concrete strength is the water-to-cement (W/C) ratio.

| W/C ratio | Strength | Workability |
|-----------|---------|------------|
| 0.40 | Very high | Stiff / hard to work |
| 0.45 | High | Moderate |
| 0.50 | Standard | Good |
| 0.55 | Moderate | Easy |
| 0.60+ | Weak | Very workable |

**Rule:** Every extra splash of water weakens the concrete. Add the minimum needed for workability.

## How many bags do I need?

- 80 lb bag = ~0.022 cubic yards = ~0.60 cubic feet
- 1 cubic yard = ~45 bags of 80 lb concrete

Use the [Concrete Calculator](/calculator/concrete) to get the exact bag count for your slab dimensions.

## Fiber reinforcement vs. rebar

| Option | Best for | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| No reinforcement | Thin patio, small walkways | Under 50 sq ft |
| Fiber additive | Patio, garage floor | Reduces surface cracking |
| Wire mesh (WWF) | Standard slabs | Cheap; moderate control |
| #3 rebar @ 18" | Driveways, footings | Best structural reinforcement |
| #4 rebar @ 12" | Heavy loads | Required for trucks/RVs |

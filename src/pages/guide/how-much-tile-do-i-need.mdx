## The quick formula

**Tiles = ceil(Area × (1 + waste%) ÷ sq ft per tile)**

## Tile size reference

| Tile size | Sq ft per tile | Tiles per 100 sq ft (straight) |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------------------|
| 4" × 4" | 0.111 | 1,109 tiles |
| 6" × 6" | 0.250 | 440 tiles |
| 3" × 6" subway | 0.125 | 880 tiles |
| 12" × 12" | 1.000 | 110 tiles |
| 18" × 18" | 2.250 | 49 tiles |
| 24" × 24" | 4.000 | 28 tiles |

*Includes 10% waste for straight lay.*

## Waste by lay pattern

| Pattern | Waste | When |
|---------|-------|------|
| Straight (parallel to walls) | **10%** | Most floors and walls |
| Diagonal (45°) | **15%** | Feature areas, small bathrooms |
| Herringbone / pattern | **15%** | Complex cuts at edges |

## Thinset and grout estimates

| Material | Coverage | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Thinset — 50 lb bag | ~40 sq ft | 3/8" notched trowel, flat surface |
| Grout — 10 lb bag (sanded) | ~50 sq ft | 1/8" joint, 12×12 tile |

Grout coverage drops significantly with larger joints or smaller tiles (more joint area per sq ft).

## How to measure

- **Floor:** length × width in feet
- **Shower walls:** each wall separately (height × width), then add together
- **Backsplash:** measure each section (length × height), add sections
- **Irregular spaces:** use the Irregular shape option in the Tile Calculator

Use the [Tile Calculator](/calculator/tile) for your exact area, tile size, and lay pattern.

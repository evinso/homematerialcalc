## The quick formula

**Volume (cu yd) = Length (ft) × Width (ft) × Depth (in) ÷ 324**

**50 lb bags = Volume (cu ft) ÷ 0.50, rounded up**

**Tons = Volume (cu yd) × Density (1.30–1.35 tons/cu yd)**

## Depth guide by project

| Project | Recommended depth |
|---------|------------------|
| Paver / brick base (bedding layer) | 1 inch |
| Leveling layer under flagstones | 1–2 inches |
| Sandbox fill | 4–6 inches |
| Artificial turf base | 1–2 inches |
